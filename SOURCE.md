# **Cards:**

Use a number randomiser (for example via Google) to generate a number between 1 & 99\.  
Vetoing a challenge results in a 10 min time-out where only walking is allowed.   
A maximum of 2 open challenges may exist at the same time and must be used within 1h. If you drew a card over 1h ago and you didn’t solve it, it counts as a lost challenge.   
Losing a challenge is not a veto (you just don’t get any coins).
