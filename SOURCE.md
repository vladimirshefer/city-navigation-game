# **Cards:**

Use a number randomiser (for example via Google) to generate a number between 1 & 99\.  
Vetoing a challenge results in a 10 min time-out where only walking is allowed.   
A maximum of 2 open challenges may exist at the same time and must be used within 1h. If you drew a card over 1h ago and you didn’t solve it, it counts as a lost challenge.   
Losing a challenge is not a veto (you just don’t get any coins).   
Recommendation: Set a timer on your phone every time you start a new challenge. (Not relevant if played via the browser app with the built-in countdown.)

## **Curses:**

Note: Walking is always allowed. Every curse means that you must leave your current mode of transportation; however, you may resume with the next transport option if your curse doesn’t forbid it. Curses MUST be cleared before fulfilling other challenges (unless you don’t have enough coins to solve a curse) or conquering new districts\! The countdown of your current challenges does not get paused by curses\!
